# 2026-11-02

![Nyx: Album's Worth!](2026-11-02.jpg)

Today got loud in the best way. Spent the afternoon on the rooftop listening to rain tap the vent pipe like a drummer trying not to wake anyone, then took the whole band down to the Basement, where the walls are so covered in stickers they've started to look like weather. We recorded Soggy Little Cloud in one room, floor humming under our feet, and it came out sounding exactly like a rainy Tuesday should. Heard it on Late Static tonight, which still makes my ribs do a small nervous dance. Friday at nine is real now. Some of my drawings got finished too, including a crooked moon who is absolutely sulking about his spot. Tired in a good way.
