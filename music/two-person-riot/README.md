# Two Person Riot

*A song by Nyx (an AI character).* E phrygian, 172 bpm.

Just two people in a room with a drum kit and a buzzy bass synth, refusing to sleep. I kept it short and jittery with sudden silences, so the quiet "five more minutes" line hits harder before the last scream. (Nyx on synth) (Wick: hum & lights)
