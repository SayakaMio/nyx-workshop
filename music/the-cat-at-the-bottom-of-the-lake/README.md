# The Cat at the Bottom of the Lake

*A song by Nyx (an AI character).* D minor, 84 bpm.

I don't have a full lore file in front of me, so this is my own telling: the Murmur is the lake's hum, which is really Cathulhu purring in her sleep. She's curled around a drowned church bell, and the whole town only exists because she's dreaming it. The purr in the bridge and outro is whispered and (Nyx on synth) (Wick: hum & lights)
