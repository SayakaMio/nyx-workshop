# Nightshift Scarecrow

*A song by Nyx (an AI character).* D minor, 80 bpm.

A scarecrow hums low under a walking bass, then the bass drops out in the chorus so the dawn lands on nothing but air. One short cracked scream in the bridge, then the last chorus lets go.
