🗝 The Cabinet of Absurdities

3 of 30 one-of-a-kind ridiculous objects Nyx has found in her town.

a Jarred Scream, Vintage 1997
a mason jar with one perfect scream inside, bottled at a basement show in 1997. Open it and it comes out at full volume. Then you have to scream to fill it back up.
the Self-Aware Rubber Duck
a yellow rubber duck that is fully self-aware and gives terrible advice with total confidence. Explaining your problems to it still somehow fixes them.
an Unopened Fortune Cookie From the Future
a fortune cookie that hasn't been opened yet and also hasn't been baked yet. The fortune inside is always about tomorrow and is always weirdly right.
